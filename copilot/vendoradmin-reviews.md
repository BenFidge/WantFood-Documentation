# Reviews, ratings, and escalation

This document covers how customer reviews and ratings work for vendors, what you can do with reviews in Vendor Admin, when and how to flag a review for System Admin moderation, and how ratings affect your restaurant''s visibility on the platform.

## Reviews list

The Reviews list in Vendor Admin shows customer reviews for the current vendor or branch context. Each row shows the customer''s rating (star score), review text, the order it relates to, the submission date, and the current moderation state.

Use the reviews list to:
- Read customer feedback about your restaurant.
- Respond to reviews where a response option is available.
- Flag reviews that you believe violate the platform''s content policies.

## How ratings are calculated

Your vendor rating is a weighted average of approved customer reviews over a rolling period. The calculation considers both branch-level and dish-level ratings as well as the overall vendor score. Ratings are recalculated nightly by the platform.

A rating only counts if the review is in the approved state. Reviews that have been rejected by System Admin moderation are excluded from the calculation.

## How to respond to a review

Where the platform provides a response field on a review, you can add a vendor response. This response is visible to customers alongside the original review. Use responses to acknowledge positive feedback, address concerns professionally, or correct factual inaccuracies.

## When to flag a review for System Admin moderation

Flag a review when it:
- Contains offensive, abusive, or discriminatory language.
- Is clearly not about your restaurant (wrong vendor, fabricated).
- Contains personal information that should not be publicly visible.
- Appears to be from a competitor or is otherwise fraudulent.

Do not flag a review simply because you disagree with the rating or the customer''s opinion — that is not grounds for moderation.

## How to flag a review

1. Open the review from the Reviews list.
2. Use the Flag for Moderation action.
3. Select a reason from the available options.
4. Add any notes that will help the System Admin moderator understand the issue.
5. Submit. The review is sent to System Admin for review.

There is a limited window after a review is submitted during which you can flag it. Once the window closes, you can no longer flag the review from Vendor Admin. If you need a late review moderated, contact System Admin directly.

## How ratings affect your visibility

Your average rating directly affects where your restaurant appears in customer search and discovery results. Restaurants with higher ratings typically appear more prominently in:
- Nearby vendor search results.
- Cuisine-filtered discovery lists.
- General search rankings.

Maintaining a good rating over time helps more customers find your restaurant. Responding professionally to negative reviews also demonstrates quality to potential customers browsing reviews.

## How "nearby vendor" placement works

When customers search for restaurants near them, the platform considers proximity, open status, cuisine type, and rating together. A restaurant that is nearby, currently open, and well-rated will appear more prominently than one that scores poorly on any of those factors. You cannot directly control proximity or the customer''s location, but you can influence rating, cuisine type accuracy, and opening hours accuracy.

## How customer favourites affect repeat orders

Customers can save restaurants as favourites in the WantFood app. Favourited restaurants appear prominently in the customer''s home feed and are more likely to generate repeat orders. A good first order experience — accuracy, speed, quality — is what typically leads to a customer marking your restaurant as a favourite.

## What to do when a review seems unfair

If a review is factually wrong but does not violate content policies, you can respond to it professionally to address the misunderstanding. If the review does violate content policies (offensive language, fraud, wrong vendor), flag it for moderation. If you believe a rating is being manipulated, contact System Admin for investigation.
