# Vendor onboarding — apply, register, and get started

This document covers how to apply to join WantFood, what happens after submitting an application, how to complete onboarding, and what to do if the invitation link does not work.

## Applying to join WantFood

New restaurants apply through the public Apply page on the Vendor Admin site. The application form asks for:
- Business name and registered address.
- Contact name and email address.
- Restaurant type and cuisine category.
- Any supporting information requested.

Complete all required fields and submit the form. You will see the Application Success page confirming the platform received your application.

## What happens after you submit an application

After submission, WantFood platform administrators review your application. This is a manual review process — there is no instant automated approval.

If your application is approved, you receive an invitation email at the address you provided on the form. The email contains a link to the onboarding landing page.

If your application is not approved, you receive a notification explaining the decision.

There is no specific timeline for the review. If you have not heard after several days, contact WantFood using the details provided at the time of application.

## Onboarding landing page

The onboarding landing page is the first page you see when you follow the invitation link. It confirms you are about to create your Vendor Admin account. Read the information on this page before proceeding.

## Onboarding registration — creating your account

The onboarding registration page asks you to set up your account credentials:
- Your name.
- A password for your Vendor Admin account (the account is linked to your email address via Entra ID).
- Any additional details required to complete account creation.

Complete the form and submit. Your account is created at this point.

## Onboarding welcome — you are in

The onboarding welcome page confirms that your account has been created and your restaurant is set up in Vendor Admin. It explains what to do next:
- Set up your payment methods (required to accept card payments).
- Build your menu.
- Configure your delivery costs.

After reading the welcome page, navigate to the Vendor Dashboard to start setting up your restaurant.

## What to do if the invitation link does not work

### Expired invitation link

Invitation links expire after a set period. If you click the link and see an error saying it is expired or invalid, contact the WantFood platform team and ask them to resend your invitation.

### Wrong portal or access error

If clicking the link takes you to an error page, confirm you are using the correct link from the invitation email. Do not try to access Vendor Admin by typing a URL directly — use only the link in the invitation email.

### Already have an account

If you have previously been invited by a different vendor or already have a WantFood account, the invitation link may conflict with your existing identity. Contact the platform team to resolve the situation — do not attempt to create a second account.

## What to do if you need to re-invite someone to your team

If a staff member needs to access your Vendor Admin and was not included in the original onboarding, use the Users or driver invitation flows within Vendor Admin depending on their role. Standard vendor staff access is managed through the vendor user model. Drivers are managed through the Drivers section.
