# Report a delivery issue

You use this workflow when a delivery cannot complete normally and the portal should record the problem.

## When you use this feature
- the delivery fails
- the delivery cannot complete safely
- support or operations need the issue recorded

## Where the workflow starts
Delivery dashboard or Delivery detail.

## How it works
1. Open the affected delivery.
2. Choose the failed or issue-reporting path.
3. Record the issue through the portal workflow.
4. Confirm that the delivery leaves the standard success path.

## Expected result
The platform records that the delivery encountered a problem and did not complete normally.
