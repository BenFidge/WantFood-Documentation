# Mark delivered

You use this workflow when you have completed the handoff and the order is now delivered.

## When you use this feature
- the customer received the order
- the final handoff is complete
- the delivery should close in a successful state

## Where the workflow starts
Delivery dashboard or Delivery detail.

## How it works
1. Open the active delivery.
2. Confirm the delivery reached the successful handoff point.
3. Mark the delivery as delivered.
4. Check that the delivery closes in the delivered state.

## Expected result
The delivery finishes as successfully delivered.
