# Onboarding registration

You use this page to enter the account details needed to finish driver onboarding.

## What you need first
- A valid driver invitation.
- The details needed to register the driver account.

## How you reach it
Onboarding landing -> registration.

## What you can do here
- enter the required registration details
- submit the onboarding form
- move into the welcome state

## What good looks like
The driver account is created and ready for first sign-in.
