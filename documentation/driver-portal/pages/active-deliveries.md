# Active deliveries

You use this page to review the deliveries that are currently assigned to you.

## What you need first
- A signed-in driver account.
- An active or recent shift.

## How you reach it
Driver dashboard -> Active deliveries.

## What you can do here
- review the currently assigned jobs
- open the delivery dashboard or detail page
- decide which delivery needs attention next

## What good looks like
You can see the live delivery workload and move into the right delivery record quickly.
