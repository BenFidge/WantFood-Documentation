# Delivery detail

You use this page to review one delivery in more detail before you change its state.

## What you need first
- A signed-in driver account.
- A delivery assigned to you or available in history.

## How you reach it
Active deliveries or Delivery history -> open one delivery.

## What you can do here
- review address and order context
- confirm the next delivery action
- check the current state before you proceed

## What good looks like
You have enough detail to continue the delivery correctly.
