# Onboarding welcome

You use this page to confirm that driver onboarding completed and to understand the next step.

## What you need first
- A completed onboarding registration.

## How you reach it
Onboarding registration -> welcome.

## What you can do here
- confirm that onboarding finished
- move into sign-in or the signed-in area
- understand what the next working step is

## What good looks like
You are ready to enter the Driver Portal working area.
