# Driver dashboard

You use this page as the main signed-in home for shift, delivery, location, and account tasks.

## What you need first
- A signed-in driver account.

## How you reach it
Driver sign-in -> dashboard.

## What you can do here
- review current shift state
- move into active deliveries and route information
- open availability or account maintenance

## What good looks like
You can start work quickly and see what needs attention.
