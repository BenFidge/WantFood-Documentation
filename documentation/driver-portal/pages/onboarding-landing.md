# Onboarding landing

You use this page to start Driver Portal onboarding from an invitation.

## What you need first
- A valid driver invitation link.

## How you reach it
Invitation link -> onboarding landing.

## What you can do here
- confirm you reached the correct driver onboarding flow
- move into registration
- understand that the flow is tied to the invitation

## What good looks like
You can begin creating the driver account that the invitation allows.
