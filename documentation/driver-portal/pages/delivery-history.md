# Delivery history

You use this page to review deliveries that are no longer active.

## What you need first
- A signed-in driver account.

## How you reach it
Driver dashboard -> Delivery history.

## What you can do here
- review past deliveries
- open an older delivery record
- use history for reference or support follow-up

## What good looks like
You can find past deliveries when active screens are no longer enough.
