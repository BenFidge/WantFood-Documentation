# Availability schedule

You use this page to maintain your weekly working availability.

## What you need first
- A signed-in driver account.

## How you reach it
Driver dashboard -> Availability schedule.

## What you can do here
- review your current weekly schedule
- update availability slots
- apply a preset if one helps you work faster

## What good looks like
Your weekly availability reflects when you expect to work.
