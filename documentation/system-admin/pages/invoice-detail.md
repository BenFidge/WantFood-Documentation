# Invoice detail

You use this page to review the full state of one invoice before you mark it paid or cancel it.

## What you need first
- A System Admin account.
- Permission to manage invoices.

## How you reach it
Invoice list -> open an invoice.

## What you can do here
- review the invoice lines and status
- confirm whether the invoice should be settled or cancelled
- capture the audit context for support or finance follow-up

## What good looks like
You can make the correct invoice decision with the record in front of you.
