# Flagged reviews list

You use this page to review customer reviews that need moderation or escalation.

## What you need first
- A System Admin account.
- Permission to moderate reviews.

## How you reach it
Home or dashboard -> Flagged reviews.

## What you can do here
- review reviews that need moderation
- resolve the moderation decision
- decide whether rating maintenance is also needed

## What good looks like
Unsafe or disputed review content is handled through a consistent moderation workflow.
