# Offer analytics

You use this page to review the performance signals for a platform offer.

## What you need first
- A System Admin account.
- Permission to manage promotions.

## How you reach it
Platform offers list -> open analytics for an offer.

## What you can do here
- review how an offer is performing
- compare the offer outcome with campaign intent
- decide whether to continue, pause, or change the offer

## What good looks like
You can judge whether the promotion is doing the job you expect.
