# Invoice list

You use this page to review invoices that the platform has already generated.

## What you need first
- A System Admin account.
- Permission to manage invoices.

## How you reach it
Home or dashboard -> Invoices.

## What you can do here
- search for an invoice
- open invoice detail
- find invoices that need payment, cancellation, or follow-up

## What good looks like
You can locate the right invoice quickly and move into the correct finance action.
