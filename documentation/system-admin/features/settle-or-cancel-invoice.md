# Settle or cancel invoice

You use this workflow when an existing invoice should be marked paid or cancelled.

## When you use this feature
- a vendor payment has completed
- an invoice should no longer remain active
- finance support needs to correct the record state

## Where the workflow starts
Invoice list -> Invoice detail.

## How it works
1. Open the invoice record.
2. Review the invoice status and lines.
3. Choose the correct settle or cancel action.
4. Confirm that the invoice now reflects the intended state.

## Expected result
The invoice record matches the real finance outcome and no longer sits in the wrong state.
