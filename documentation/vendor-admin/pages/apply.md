# Apply

You use this page to submit a new restaurant application to join WantFood.

## What you need first
- A prospective vendor business.
- The restaurant and contact details you want to submit.

## How you reach it
Vendor Admin public apply route.

## What you can do here
- enter the business details
- submit the application for review
- move into the application success page

## What good looks like
The platform records the application and moves it into the admin review flow.
