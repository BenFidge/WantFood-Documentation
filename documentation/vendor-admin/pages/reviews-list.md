# Reviews list

You use this page to review customer feedback and escalate issues that need platform moderation.

## What you need first
- A signed-in vendor-linked account.
- The correct vendor or branch context.

## How you reach it
Vendor dashboard -> Reviews.

## What you can do here
- review customer feedback
- identify reviews that need escalation
- flag a review for System Admin follow-up

## What good looks like
You can keep an eye on customer feedback and escalate content that needs admin review.
