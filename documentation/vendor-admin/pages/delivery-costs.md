# Delivery costs

You use this page to manage delivery pricing for the current vendor or branch context.

## What you need first
- A signed-in vendor-linked account.
- The correct vendor or branch context.

## How you reach it
Vendor dashboard -> Delivery costs.

## What you can do here
- review current delivery pricing
- update delivery cost settings
- save changes that should affect future customer quotes

## What good looks like
Delivery pricing matches the current trading rules for the branch or vendor.
