# Drivers list

You use this page to manage the drivers linked to the current vendor or branch.

## What you need first
- A signed-in vendor-linked account.
- The correct vendor or branch context.

## How you reach it
Vendor dashboard -> Drivers list.

## What you can do here
- review current drivers
- invite a new driver
- open a driver record for maintenance or branch assignment

## What good looks like
Driver access and branch relationships stay current for dispatch operations.
