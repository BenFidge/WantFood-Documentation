# Manage restaurant

You use this page to maintain the restaurant profile and branch-facing information for the current context.

## What you need first
- A signed-in vendor-linked account.
- The correct vendor or branch context.

## How you reach it
Vendor dashboard -> Manage restaurant.

## What you can do here
- review the current restaurant profile
- update editable restaurant details
- maintain branch-specific information from the same working area

## What good looks like
The vendor and branch information stays current for operations and downstream customer-facing use.
