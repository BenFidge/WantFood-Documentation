# Team list

You use this page to manage the staff members who have access to the Vendor Admin site under the current vendor account.

## What you need first
- A signed-in vendor-linked account with Owner or Manager role.
- The correct vendor context selected.

## How you reach it
Vendor dashboard → Team.

## What you can do here
- review all current team members for this vendor
- see each member's name, email address, role, and the date their account was added
- add a new team member — you are taken to the [Account Created](account-created.md) screen after a successful add
- remove a team member who no longer needs access

## What good looks like
Only the people who actively work at this vendor have access to Vendor Admin, and each person has the correct role.
