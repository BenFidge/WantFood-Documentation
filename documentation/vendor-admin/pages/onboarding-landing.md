# Onboarding landing

You use this page to start the onboarding journey after your application is approved.

## What you need first
- An approved application.
- A valid onboarding invitation or link.

## How you reach it
Invitation link -> onboarding landing.

## What you can do here
- confirm you have reached the right onboarding flow
- continue into registration
- understand that this flow is tied to your invitation

## What good looks like
You can begin account registration for the approved vendor.
