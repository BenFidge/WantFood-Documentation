# Driver onboarding handoff

You use this workflow to understand how a Vendor Admin invitation becomes a Driver Portal onboarding journey.

## When you use this feature
- you have just invited a driver
- a driver says they did not complete onboarding
- you need to explain what the driver should do next

## Where the workflow starts
Drivers list -> invite driver.

## How it works
1. Create the driver invitation from Vendor Admin.
2. Confirm that the invitation is issued.
3. Direct the driver into the Driver Portal onboarding flow.
4. Recheck the driver record after the driver completes onboarding.

## Expected result
The invited driver can move from invitation into a working Driver Portal account.
